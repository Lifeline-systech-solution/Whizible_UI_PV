e-Dashboard

  • A visual representation of critical operating parameters such as Resource Utilization, Schedule & Effort Variance
  • The e-Dashboards in WHIZIBLE are role based, and can be configured for each individual user.
  • Drill down facility enables the user to view the information to required level of detail.
  • Information can be presented using 11 types of graphs
  • Generates Alerts triggered by events moving into critical ranges. These Alerts can be in the form of emails sent to concerned resources
  • Frequently used reports can be accessed directly from e-Dashboard

Projects

  • Create Project Management Plan which includes all details about the project such as Phases, Modules, Process, Resources, Quantitative Objectives, Training Plan, Risk Management Plan, Milestones, Change Management etc.
  • Manage project execution – assign tasks to resources and track their progress.
  • Project Management & Tracking to monitor adherence to plans. Reviews of project progress
  • Project Closure and Analysis, including Lessons Learnt
  • Project related Measurements and Reports

Process

  • Provides a facility to capture the Organization’s Quality Management System, including processes, forms, documents, templates, guidelines and checklists.
  • Select software processes at project level to suit project lifecycle.
  • Define Process Management Indicators and corresponding metrics
  • Analyze milestones with the help of reports and graphs and decide the health of the project execution
  • Capture deviations against organization values and planned quantitative objectives.

Issue

  • Repository of issues raised or encountered by various stakeholders of a project.
  • Systematic log of issues, actions taken and resolution
  • Role based access and modification rights
  • Issue configuration at project level and organization level, so that trends across multiple projects can be analyzed.

Help Desk

  • Designed to fulfill support queries of customers or internal stakeholders.
  • Requests can be converted to tasks and assigned to resources
  • Discussion threads related to the requests are maintained for future references

Knowledge

  • A repository for knowledge in the organization, across multiple teams
  • Can also be used to maintain a library of QMS documents
  • Reusable elements are effectively maintained and shared, preventing repetition of efforts
  • Specific and comprehensive search features, including filters

Resources

  • Contains master information about the resources in the organization
  • Maintains an inventory of employee skill sets, which can be updated when the resource is working in / released from a project
  • Manage HR activities including goal setting, reviews, billing goals and priorities

Reports / Metrics

  • Contains standard reports, and the ability to create custom reports using the Query Builder.
  • Reports can be exported in various formats such as XML, CSV, PDF, RTF, Excel, Text, HTML.
  • WHIZIBLE comes with over 50 standard reports that cover a wide variety of business data requirements, including Task Report, Measurement Report, Utilization Report and Resource Availability Report.

Billing

  • Manage all aspects of project accounting including timesheet authentication, invoice generation, payment receipts.
  • Invoice generation based on authenticated timesheets
  • Generate invoices for Time & Material, Fixed Rate or Fixed Bid projects

Timesheet

  • Data capture point for activities, work hours and tasks
  • Can track activity based as well as task based timesheets
  • Task Report displays daily activity details for a specified period
  • Weekly view provided for quick entry of time
